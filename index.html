How to Play

Paint cells on the void. They live and die by B3/S23: a dead cell with three neighbors is born; a live cell with two or three stays; everyone else dies.

  • Drag to paint. Shift or right-drag to erase. Touch works.
  • Challenges give you a budget — only that many live cells before you hit Run.
  • Space pauses. . steps one generation. U undoes. N cycles stamps. [ ] change speed.
  • Space-drag or middle-drag pans. Wheel zooms. Esc opens settings.
